Common questions

My brief didn't appear in the morning

Morning Report refreshes overnight using iOS background tasks, which iOS schedules at its own discretion — typically when your phone is charging and on Wi-Fi. If briefs are unreliable, check that Background App Refresh is enabled for Morning Report in iOS Settings. You can always pull to refresh in the app to build a brief immediately.

Weather isn't showing

Weather requires location access. In iOS Settings under Morning Report, confirm Location is set to While Using the App. Weather is unavailable in the iOS Simulator by design.

My calendar events or reminders are missing

Confirm the source is enabled in the app's Settings, and that Calendar and Reminders access are granted in iOS Settings. The brief shows today's events, and reminders that are due today or overdue — not your full lists.

Can Morning Report send email or change my inbox?

No. It requests read-only Gmail access and is technically incapable of sending, deleting, archiving, or modifying anything in your mailbox. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect my Google account?

Sign out in the app's Settings. To revoke access completely, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Morning Report.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything it stores is local to your device, so removing the app removes all of it. There is no account and no server-side copy.
