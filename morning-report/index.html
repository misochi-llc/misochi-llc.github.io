Morning Report

An iOS app that assembles one brief for the day ahead — today's weather, your calendar, the reminders that are due, and the handful of emails actually worth knowing about — and has it waiting when you wake up.

It runs on your device. There is no Misochi account, no server holding your data, and no analytics. Email prioritization happens on-device using Apple's on-device models. Nothing you connect is transmitted to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.
